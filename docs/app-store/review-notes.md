## Notes

```text
Photos Cleanup helps clear unwanted photos from the Photos library. It suggests screenshots, bursts, similar shots, blurry shots and short videos to delete, and files the user's decisions into albums. It never deletes on its own.

PERMISSION
The app needs Photos access with Full Access (read and write). Please choose Allow Full Access at the first prompt. If access is denied or limited, the app shows "No access to Photos" with an Open Privacy Settings button, and nothing else works. The app has no network code and sends no data anywhere.

TRY IT (about two minutes)
1. In the sidebar under Review, pick Screenshots, Bursts, Videos or Low Quality. Wait for the progress bar to finish; the grid fills in. Each pass lists photos in sections (a month, or a group of shots).
2. Photos marked Delete show a delete badge; the rest are Keep. Select a photo and press K to keep or the Delete key to mark delete.
3. Click a section's Confirm button (or press Return). The section leaves the pass and moves to "Not in Photos Yet". Nothing has changed in Photos at this point, and Undo (toolbar, Cmd-Z) reverses it. "Confirm All" in the toolbar does this for every section.
4. Click "Apply N to Photos…" and then Apply. Photos are now filed into albums in the Photos app: "<Pass title> - Delete Me" for photos marked delete and "<Pass title> - Keep" for the rest, for example "Screenshots - Delete Me" and "Screenshots - Keep". The Photos app itself shows these albums.
5. Open "In Delete Me Albums" in the sidebar. Click "Delete N in Photos…". macOS shows its own confirmation dialog. Those photos are deleted only if the user confirms there. Photos keeps them in Recently Deleted for 30 days. To avoid deleting anything during review, stop after step 4, or cancel the macOS dialog.

The app deletes only photos in the Delete Me albums, and only through that macOS dialog. It never changes or deletes anything else. Favorites are never suggested, and by default neither are photos in the user's own albums.

WHAT A SMALL LIBRARY SHOWS
A pass is empty when the library has nothing for it. Most likely to have content in any library:
- Screenshots: lists every screenshot.
- Videos: lists every video, longest first (clips under 2 seconds start as delete).
- Bursts: two or more shots within a minute of each other. Similar: shots of the same scene on the same day.
- Low Quality: blurry or dark shots; often empty.
- All lists every photo by month, whether or not a pass suggests it.
Importing a few screenshots and a few quick back-to-back photos into Photos is enough to fill Screenshots and Bursts.

macOS 27 AND APPLE INTELLIGENCE
Screenshots and Paperwork use Apple's on-device Foundation Models, which need macOS 27 or later with Apple Intelligence on. Without it, Screenshots still lists the screenshots but every one starts as Keep (the user can mark them by hand), Paperwork stays empty, and a note under the grid says why. Everything else (Bursts, Similar, Low Quality, Videos, All, Export, Import) works on macOS 15 or later using Vision, with no Apple Intelligence.

EXPORT AND IMPORT (sidebar, under Transfer)
Export copies photos and videos to a folder the user picks. Import sorts a folder the user picks into Done and Import subfolders on disk. Both touch only folders chosen in the macOS open panel, and never delete anything or change the Photos library.
```
